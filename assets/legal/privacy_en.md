This policy explains which personal data is processed in the Stelinda app and on stelinda.com, why it is processed and what rights users have. It is the privacy notice under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

## Data controller

Kerem Aydın
Email: destek@stelinda.com

## Data we process

- Account details: first name, last name, email address, username and password. The password is stored in a form that cannot be reversed; no one can see it.
- Profile picture: the default picture assigned at sign-up; with Google sign-in, the photo on your Google account.
- With Google or Apple sign-in: that account's identifier, email address and name. If you hide your email with Apple, the relay address Apple provides.
- Learning data: your answers, progress on words, points, league points and rank, streak, daily activity, quests and tickets.
- Subscription details: the App Store purchase record (transaction ID, subscription type, expiry date). Apple takes the payment; we have no access to card or payment details.
- Report and block records: the fact that a user was reported or blocked, the reason for the report and its date.
- Support correspondence: emails sent to destek@stelinda.com.
- Kept only on your device: session keys, sound and vibration preferences, and reminder settings. These are not sent to the server.

## Why we process it

- To create your account and let you sign in.
- To save your learning progress and show you suitable words and reviews.
- To build leagues and rankings.
- To verify your Premium subscription and track its period.
- To review reports, hide blocked users from rankings and enforce the community rules.
- To show a daily reminder if you allow it. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- To answer support requests, keep the service secure and fix errors.

Data is processed to the extent necessary to enter into and perform the terms of use (KVKK Art. 5/2-c) and on the basis of legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).

## Who we share data with

- Other users in the same league see your first name, last name, profile picture and league points in the rankings. Users who block each other do not see each other in the rankings.
- Data is processed on servers outside Türkiye by providers that help deliver the service: Neon for the database (Germany); Amazon Web Services for profile pictures, card images and sounds (Sweden); Google Cloud for the app's server (Belgium); Cloudflare for the website and forwarding support emails.
- With Google or Apple sign-in, information is exchanged with that company to verify your sign-in.
- When you buy a subscription, Apple takes the payment and notifies Stelinda's server of the purchase and renewals.
- Data is not sold and is not used for advertising. The app contains no advertising or usage analytics tools.
- If a legal obligation arises, data may be shared with the competent authorities.

## How long we keep it

Data is kept for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account, the account and all data linked to it (including learning, subscription, report and block records) are deleted immediately and permanently. It may remain in providers' technical backups for a short while and is then deleted automatically. Support correspondence is kept for at most one year after the request is resolved.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose, to know the parties it is transferred to, to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate, to request its deletion or destruction, to object to processing and to claim compensation for damages. Residents of the European Union also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability and objection under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Requests are made from the email address registered to your account to destek@stelinda.com and are answered within 30 days at the latest. If you find the answer insufficient, you can file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority.

## Cookies

stelinda.com does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our infrastructure provider Cloudflare may use strictly necessary cookies for security.

## Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version is published in the app and at stelinda.com/privacy; the date is shown at the top.
